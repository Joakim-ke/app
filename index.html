About SmartBudget

SmartBudget is a personal finance manager designed for Kenyan users. It helps you track income, expenses, budgets, savings, and debts.

Privacy Notice: All data is stored in your browser's LocalStorage. No data is sent to any server. If you clear your browser cache, you will lose your data unless you export it first.
